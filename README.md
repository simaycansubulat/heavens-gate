# Heaven’s Gate

**Play it here: https://simaycansubulat.github.io/heavens-gate/**

You are dead. You climb an endless staircase through the clouds to a golden gate, and the gate decides where you go: Heaven, a meadow on floating islands, or Hell, a burning ruined city. She is sassy, unfair, and impossible to predict.

There are two ways to play:

- **Face the gate.** She looks at you through your camera. She comments on what she sees (your top, a colour on you, your hair, something in your room), gives you an order (“Smile. Big smile.”), has one of her moments (déjà vu, mistaken identity, a crush on your plant…), and judges you. Sometimes she just bursts out laughing at your face first.
- **Choose your character.** Pick a person or an animal, watch them climb, and answer her questions.

Every verdict sets off one big event in the place the soul went (“It started to rain candy in Heaven.”), announced by a deep narrator voice. Whatever it creates stays in the world. She rarely repeats herself, and she remembers what she has said even after a reload.

## Run it

The whole game is one file, `index.html`. Open the link above, or double-click the file. Use Chrome or Edge for the best voices.

- **Camera:** only used for “Face the gate”. Everything stays in your browser: frames are shrunk, averaged and thrown away, and nothing is uploaded. The object spotting uses TensorFlow.js and COCO-SSD, loaded from jsDelivr.
- **Voices** use the browser’s built-in speech, so they depend on the voices your computer has installed. Untick **Voices** to mute them; the subtitles always show what is said.

## Repainting the scenery

The painted world is generated by `tools/bake_scenery.py` (needs numpy, scipy and Pillow). It writes `assets/world.jpg`, `assets/door-left.png` and `js/geometry.js`; their contents then have to be copied into `index.html`, where the images are embedded and the geometry lives in its own script block.
